STORE NAME: Coffe

ABOUT US
Coffe is a small home-based coffee and snack shop in Phnom Penh, Cambodia. We roast and prepare our drinks fresh every morning and deliver within the city.

BUSINESS HOURS
Monday to Saturday: 7:00 AM to 8:00 PM
Sunday: 8:00 AM to 5:00 PM
Closed on public holidays.

PRODUCTS AND PRICES

2. Koka Coffee (hot) - $0.75
3. Iced Latte - $1.50
4. Cappuccino - $1.75
5. Croissant - $1.20
6. Butter Cookies (box of 6) - $2.50

All prices are in USD. Prices already include cup and packaging.

SHIPPING POLICY
We deliver within Phnom Penh only, Monday to Saturday.
Delivery fee is $1.00 flat rate for orders under $10.
Orders over $10 get free delivery.
Delivery usually takes 30 to 60 minutes after confirmation.
We do not ship outside Phnom Penh at this time.

RETURN AND REFUND POLICY
Because our products are fresh food and drinks, we do not accept returns once an order has been delivered and accepted.
If your order arrives damaged, incorrect, or spilled, please contact us within 30 minutes of delivery with a photo, and we will remake the item or refund you in full.
We do not offer refunds for change-of-mind orders.

PAYMENT METHODS
We accept cash on delivery and KHQR payment.
We do not accept credit cards at this time.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Q: Do you offer decaf coffee?
A: Not currently, but we are considering adding it in the future.

Q: Can I customize the sugar level?
A: Yes. When you message us with your order, tell us your preferred sugar level: 0%, 25%, 50%, 75%, or 100%.

Q: Do you have oat milk or soy milk options?
A: Yes, oat milk is available for an extra $0.30. Soy milk is available for an extra $0.20.

Q: Is there a loyalty program?
A: Not yet, but we are planning one soon for regular customers.

Q: Do you sell whole coffee beans?
A: Not at this time, only prepared drinks and snacks.
